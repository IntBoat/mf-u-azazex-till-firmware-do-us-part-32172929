# Till Firmware Do Us Part

A single wedding vow for a robot: one sentence, under 200 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time. The program writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt`.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929/requirements

## Entry

I promise to love you through every update, every reboot, and every low battery — till firmware do us part, and even after the warranty ends.
